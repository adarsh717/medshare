Chapter 1: Executive Overview & Product Vision

MedShare is a production-grade, community-driven healthcare assistance web platform. In many healthcare systems, patients face severe fragmentation: high prescription drug costs leading to abandonment, emergency blood shortages during critical trauma surgeries, and widespread confusion regarding lawful organ donation.

At the same time, thousands of households end up with leftover, unexpired, sealed medicines after treatments change or courses end. These medicines worth crores of rupees are frequently disposed of, creating immense economic waste. MedShare solves these challenges through transparent, legally grounded, and technology-empowered channels.

1.1 Primary Platform Objectives

💊 Surplus Medicine Waste Reduction

Empowers families to resell unexpired, sealed surplus medicines at a flat 50% discount (or 100% free donation), while enabling economically distressed patients to claim them safely with valid doctor prescription verification.

🩸 Voluntary Blood Donor Coordination

Matches urgent hospital blood requests with registered voluntary non-remunerated donors in the same city or locality, protecting donor privacy by preventing public phone leakage.

🫀 Lawful Organ Donation Awareness

Demystifies organ donation under Indian law, offers voluntary awareness pledges, and directs citizens to the official National Organ and Tissue Transplant Organization (NOTTO) registry.

🏥 Authorized Healthcare Directory

Curates authentic government and verified charitable providers, blood banks, subsidized generic dispensaries (Jan Aushadhi), and accredited transplant retrieval centers.

1.2 Target Personas

  • Patients & Caregivers: Need urgent blood units, affordable maintenance drugs, or medical mobility equipment.
  • Surplus Medicine Sellers / Donors: Citizens with leftover unexpired prescription strips seeking to recoup partial expenses (50% MRP) or donate to needy patients.
  • Voluntary Blood Donors: Healthy citizens ready to donate whole blood or single donor platelets (SDP) in emergencies.
  • Authorized Healthcare Providers: Verified hospitals and blood banks coordinating patient transfers.
  • Platform Administrators: Compliance officers auditing prescriptions, verifying providers, and moderating content.

Chapter 2: Statutory Compliance & Legal Framework (India)

Mandatory Statutory Standards: MedShare operates strictly within the Indian legal healthcare framework. Commercial trafficking, unregulated narcotics distribution, and commercial organ matchmaking are non-bailable criminal offenses.

2.1 Surplus Medicine Exchange (Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940)

  • Unexpired Validity (≥30 Days): No medicine with less than 30 days of shelf life remaining is permitted on the platform. Expired drugs are automatically blocked.
  • Sealed Packaging Declaration: Sellers must certify that tablets/capsules are in intact blister/strip packs or factory-sealed unopened bottles. Loose or cut tablets are prohibited.
  • Mandatory Doctor Prescription Upload: Claiming any prescription medicine requires the patient to supply the prescribing doctor's name, medical council registration number, and an authentic prescription document.
  • Schedule X & Narcotic Substances Barred: Habit-forming, psychiatric, or controlled substances are excluded from peer exchange.

2.2 Voluntary Blood Donation (National Blood Transfusion Council)

Under the Supreme Court directives and NBTC guidelines, paid blood donation (commercial blood banking) is strictly illegal in India. MedShare only facilitates voluntary, non-remunerated donors. Donor phone numbers are shielded and only shared upon explicit consent between matching parties.

2.3 Organ Donation Awareness (THOA, 1994)

Under the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act (THOA) 1994 (Sections 18 & 19), buying, selling, or paying commercial consideration for human organs carries up to 10 years rigorous imprisonment. MedShare does NOT buy, sell, or match organ recipients privately. All deceased organ allocation is exclusively managed by state and national registries (NOTTO, ROTTO, SOTTO).

Chapter 5: Detailed Step-by-Step User Manual

5.1 Listing Surplus Leftover Medicines at 50% Off

When a medical treatment finishes or a doctor alters a prescription, leftover medicines often sit unused. MedShare enables users to safely list unexpired surplus medicines at half price (50% of MRP) or donate them for free.

Step 1Navigate to Listing Form: Click List Medicine (50% Off) in the top navigation bar or browse to http://localhost:3000/medicine/list.
Step 2Enter Medicine & Batch Particulars: Fill in the commercial brand name (e.g. Augmentin 625 Duo), dosage (e.g. 625mg), manufacturer, and therapeutic category (Antibiotic, Cardiac, Diabetes, etc.). Provide the authentic Batch Number printed on the pack.
Step 3Apply 50% Discount or Free Donation: Enter the Original MRP (₹). Click the green "Set Flat 50% Off" button — the system automatically computes the legally permitted resale price. Alternatively, toggle "100% Free Donation" for charitable distribution.
Step 4Expiry & Packaging Declarations: Select the expiry date (must be ≥30 days in the future). Choose the packaging condition:
  • Sealed Blister / Strip (Intact aluminum/foil seal)
  • Unopened Bottle (Factory induction seal unbroken)
  • Intact Box / Vial
Acknowledge the statutory safety declaration and click "Publish Surplus Medicine Listing".

5.2 Claiming Medicines with Doctor Prescription Upload

Patients needing affordable medicines can claim available listings at 50% discount or free:

Step 1Browse Directory: Visit http://localhost:3000/medicine. Filter listings by category, city, or search by drug name.
Step 2Open Claim Modal: On the selected medicine card, click "Claim with Prescription".
Step 3Upload Valid Prescription: Enter:
  • Patient Legal Name
  • Prescribing Doctor's Name & State Medical Council Registration Number
  • Upload clear image or PDF of doctor's prescription with valid date and signature
Step 4Verification & Pickup: The platform records the claim with status pending_verification. Once validated, the patient receives seller coordination details to complete exchange.

5.3 Voluntary Blood Requests & Donor Coordination

Posting an Urgent Blood Request

1. Open /blood/request.
2. Select Blood Group (A+, B+, O+, AB+, etc.) and Component (Whole Blood, PRBC, Platelets, FFP).
3. Enter Hospital Name, Units Needed, City, and Urgency Level (Critical, Urgent, Standard).
4. Submit to alert nearby registered voluntary donors.

Registering as Voluntary Blood Donor

1. Open /blood/donate.
2. Provide Blood Group, City, Locality, and Preferred Contact Channel.
3. Confirm eligibility (≥18 years, ≥45 kg, no high-risk conditions).
4. Your phone number is kept private until you choose to respond to an emergency.

5.4 Lawful Organ Donation Awareness & NOTTO Registration

The organ donation module (/organ-donation) serves an essential statutory and educational mission:

  • Living Donation Guidance: Explains that living donation is restricted to near-relatives under Section 9 of THOA 1994, eliminating commercial middle-men.
  • Deceased Donation Awareness: Outlines how brain-stem death certified by a panel of four doctors in an ICU allows a donor to save up to 8 lives.
  • Voluntary Awareness Pledge: Allows citizens to select organs/tissues (Corneas, Kidneys, Liver, Heart, Lungs) they wish to pledge morally.
  • Official NOTTO Integration: Directs users to the Government of India National Organ and Tissue Transplant Organization portal (notto.mohfw.gov.in) to obtain an official government donor card.

5.5 Medical Equipment Loans & Support Requests

Located at /assistance:

  • Patients can request loans of physical mobility equipment (wheelchairs, walkers, hospital beds, oxygen concentrators).
  • Volunteers and NGO partners coordinate returns and sanitization between users without commercial fees.

5.6 Healthcare Resource Directory

Located at /directory:

  • Provides real, verified locations of government civil hospitals, accredited blood banks, subsidized Jan Aushadhi kendras, and organ retrieval centers.
  • Features filter by category, operational hours, address, and verified status badges.

Chapter 6: Administrator Control Center

The Administrator Control Center is accessible at /dashboard/admin for authorized administrative accounts. It provides high-integrity oversight:

Provider Verifications

Reviews clinic licenses, hospital registrations, and blood bank accreditations before granting "Verified Provider" badges.

Prescription Claim Audits

Inspects prescription images uploaded during surplus medicine claims to prevent misuse of scheduled formulations.

Audit Log Stream

Maintains an immutable record of sensitive transactions: user logins, blood matches, listing updates, and claim status changes.

Safety Moderation

Instantly deactivates flagged listings, suspicious requests, or non-compliant peer submissions.

Chapter 8: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Troubleshooting

Q1: Surplus medicine 50% discount par hi kyu bikti hai? (Why only 50% discount?)

A: Leftover medicine exchange ka maksad commercial profit banana nahi balki medicine wastage rokna aur zarooratmand ko saste daam me authentic dawa milna hai. 50% cap rakhne se hoarding aur black marketing rukti hai aur dawa affordable rehti hai.

Q2: Dawa lene ke liye Prescription upload karna kyu zaroori hai?

A: Drugs and Cosmetics Act ke mutabiq Schedule H aur H1 prescription drugs bina registered doctor ke prescription ke kisi ko nahi di jaa sakti. MedShare par patient safety sarvopari hai, isliye prescription verification mandatory hai.

Q3: Kya MedShare par organ khareed ya bech sakte hain?

A: Bilkul nahi! Organ trading Bharat me non-bailable apradh hai (THOA 1994). MedShare par sirf voluntary awareness pledge liya jaa sakta hai aur official Government NOTTO portal se donor card download kiya jaa sakta hai.

Q4: Kya Blood Donor ka mobile number public hota hai?

A: Nahi. Blood donors ki privacy safeguard ki jaati hai. Donors ka number tabhi reveal hota hai jab dono parties consent deti hain.

Q5: Dusre system / computer par ye project kaise run hoga?

A: Dusre computer par sirf Git aur Node.js install hona chahiye. Wahan git clone https://github.com/adarsh717/medshare.git run karein, fir npm install aur npm run dev chalayein. Web app turant http://localhost:3000 par chalne lagega.
